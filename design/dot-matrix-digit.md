## Reusable digit display

**Name:** DotDigit

**Job:** Displays one digit on a five-column, seven-row dot grid.

**Input:** A prepared pattern consisting of seven strings, each containing five `1`/`0` characters; `1` turns a dot on and `0` turns it off.

**Zero pattern:**

```text
01110
10001
10001
10001
10001
10001
01110
```
