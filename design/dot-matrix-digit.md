## Reusable digit display

**Name:** DotDigit

**Job:** Displays one digit on a five-column, seven-row dot grid.

**Input:** A single flat string of 35 `1`/`0` characters, ordered row by row from top to bottom, with five characters per row; `1` turns a dot on and `0` turns it off.

**Zero pattern:**

```text
01110
10001
10001
10001
10001
10001
01110
```
